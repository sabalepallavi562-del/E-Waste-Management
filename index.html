PRIVACY & SECURITY

Protect Your Data

Your electronic devices may contain personal photos, documents, passwords and other private information. Always protect your data before recycling or donating an electronic device.

🔐 Backup Save important files before disposal.
🗑️ Remove Personal Data Delete personal files and accounts.
🔄 Factory Reset Reset supported devices before handing them over.
🛡️ Data Handling Do not share passwords, OTPs or sensitive information with unknown people.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is e-waste?

E-waste means discarded electrical and electronic equipment such as phones, computers, televisions, batteries and electronic accessories.

Why should e-waste be recycled?

Recycling helps recover useful materials and reduces environmental pollution caused by improper disposal.

Can I recycle a working device?

Yes. However, reuse, repair or donation may be better options when the device is still working.

Is my pickup request stored?

In this project demo, pickup information is stored locally in your browser using localStorage.
