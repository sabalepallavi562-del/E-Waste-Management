About E-Waste

E-waste means discarded electronic devices such as mobile phones, computers, televisions, batteries and chargers. Proper management helps reduce pollution and recover useful materials.

📉

Reduce

Reduce unnecessary electronic purchases and use devices for a longer period.

🔄

Reuse

Reuse, repair or donate working electronic devices instead of throwing them away.

♻️

Recycle

Send unwanted electronic devices to appropriate recycling or collection facilities.

Recycling Tips

Follow these simple tips before recycling your electronics.

🔒

Protect Your Data

Delete personal information and perform a factory reset before recycling devices.

🔋

Handle Batteries Carefully

Do not throw batteries into normal household waste.

♻️

Use Recycling Centers

Give electronic waste to proper recycling or collection facilities.

📦

Keep Devices Safe

Store old electronics safely until they are given for recycling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is E-Waste?

E-waste is electronic equipment that is discarded or no longer needed.

Why should we recycle E-Waste?

Recycling helps recover useful materials and reduces environmental pollution.

Can mobile phones be recycled?

Yes. Old mobile phones can be given to suitable electronic recycling facilities.

Can batteries be recycled?

Yes. Batteries should be handled and recycled through appropriate collection systems.

Is data deletion important?

Yes. Personal data should be removed before giving an old device for recycling.

How can I schedule a pickup?

Fill out the Schedule Pickup form on this website.
